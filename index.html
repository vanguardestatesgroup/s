04How it works

From enquiry to keys — the whole route.

A five-stage process built for buyers who live in a different country from the home they are buying.

01

Enquiry & matching

Tell us your budget, preferred region and whether you want the monthly plan or bulk payment. Within one working day you receive a shortlist of matched homes with photographs, floor areas, plot sizes and catalogue prices.

02

Reservation & inspection fee

You reserve the home with a $200 inspection fee. That is the entire upfront cost — no deposit, no reservation levy, no administrative charge. The fee is refunded when the purchase completes.

03

Survey, title search & walkthrough

Our surveyor attends the property within five working days. You receive a written condition report, a title registry search and a narrated video walkthrough. If the report does not satisfy you, the file closes and the fee is returned.

04

Contract & payment

Choose the $5,000 monthly plan or bulk payment with 4% off. Both are set out in one plain-language contract. Monthly clients begin instalments on a date they choose; bulk clients settle through escrow or wire.

05

Documentation

Our legal team prepares the deed transfer, files with the local registry and arranges apostilled copies for international buyers. Originals are couriered with tracking, anywhere in the world, at our cost.

06

Handover

Keys are released in person or by our delivery partner, with a live video handover for remote buyers. Monthly clients receive keys at 60% paid; bulk clients at transfer. Aftercare covers utilities, insurance and local management introductions.

05Frequently asked

Straight answers.

Is the $200 inspection fee refundable?
Yes. It is refunded in full in two situations: if the property fails our inspection and you walk away, or when you complete the purchase. It is the only payment taken before you receive the property report.
How does the $5,000 monthly plan actually work?
The instalment is fixed at $5,000 regardless of the home. The number of months is simply the catalogue price divided by $5,000 and rounded up. A $35,000 home runs 7 months; a $120,000 home runs 24 months. There is no interest, no arrangement fee and no indexation.
What discount do I get for paying in bulk?
4% off the catalogue price. On a $120,000 home that is a saving of $4,800. Bulk buyers also receive the fastest timeline: title deeds and keys within 21 working days of cleared funds.
Can I buy from outside the United States?
Absolutely — distance is not a barrier. Roughly seven in ten of our buyers are outside the country where the property sits. Everything from the video walkthrough to the deed courier is designed for remote purchase, and all prices are quoted in US dollars.
What is the cheapest home you sell?
Our catalogue starts at $35,000 for a completed, title-verified home. Entry-level properties are typically one and two bedroom houses in regional towns, and they are subject to the same inspection standard as our villas.
When do I get the keys?
Bulk buyers at transfer, usually 21 working days after funds clear. Monthly plan buyers at 60% of the balance paid, with full title transferred once the final instalment settles.
What happens if a property fails inspection?
The file closes, you receive the written report explaining the fault, and every payment — including the $200 fee — is returned. We would rather lose a sale than hand over a home we would not live in ourselves.
